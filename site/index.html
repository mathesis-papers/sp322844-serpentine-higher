Rank-two extensions of Spezia's 2018 determinant result: the two conjectures in OEIS A322844, Comments (revision 68)

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Abstract

Extending Spezia's determinant result (2018), we prove his two conjectures in OEIS A322844, Comments, the two statements in the conjectures block (revision 68, August 23, 2025): the absolute second exterior trace of the serpentine matrix is the stated quartic quasipolynomial, and every exterior trace of order greater than two vanishes. The matrix has rank exactly two for every size at least two. A factorization through a two-dimensional space gives its characteristic polynomial, including the sign of the second exterior trace. Corollaries give a minimal polynomial, a permanent formula, diagonal sums, generating functions and reciprocal sums. We distinguish the exponential generating function from a shifted-factorial expression in the source.
